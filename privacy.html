Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Information you provide

When you contact Cat Masters Studio, you may voluntarily provide your name, email address, order details, and anything you include in your message. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to answer your question.

Email addresses and forms

The current waitlist form displays a confirmation only. It does not send or save your email address. If email collection is connected later, this policy will be updated to explain the service and how submitted addresses are used. Emails you send directly to us may be retained to respond to you.

Purchases and payments

Payments are processed by Stripe through its checkout service. Cat Masters Studio does not directly collect or store your full credit card details. We may receive purchase information through Stripe, such as your name, email address, purchased product, payment status, and transaction details, to fulfill orders and provide support. Stripe handles payment information under its own Privacy Policy.

How information is used

Information may be used to deliver purchases, resolve download or payment issues, answer questions, maintain transaction records, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. If you opt into updates when an email service is added, your address may be used for those updates, with a way to unsubscribe. Cat Masters Studio does not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This website currently does not add analytics tools or tracking cookies. Third-party services such as Stripe may use cookies on their own sites. If analytics or cookies are added here later, this policy will explain their purpose and any choices or consent required by applicable law.

Third-party services

We use Stripe for payments. Hosting providers and email providers may process information needed to run the website or handle correspondence, such as technical request logs or messages. Information is shared only as needed for these purposes or where required by law. External sites and services have their own privacy practices.

Security and retention

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no internet service is completely secure. Information is kept only as needed for purchases, support, recordkeeping, or applicable legal requirements.

Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us to make a request. Some transaction records may need to be retained by law. For information held independently by Stripe, you may also contact Stripe directly.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when our practices or services change. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page. Where required, we will give notice of material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@catmastersstudio.com.
